1. Create the policy with permission for accessing the aws secret manager 
    can read the value permission you can provide.
2. associate your cluster with the OIDC ( Open ID Connect ). Where it generates the unique code for
    your cluster.

    eksctl utils associate-iam-oidc-provider --cluster roboshopcluster --approve
3. Now create the service account 

    eksctl create iamserviceaccount --cluster roboshopcluster --name secret-reader --namespace roboshop --attach-policy-arn <arn> --approve

    This command will create the service account and IAM role and attach the policy and also with help of OIDC attach the trust policy as well to the role.

Why trust policy ?
===========
Because IAM should know from which cluster i am getting the request that to prove job is of OIDC
and then what was role and policy attached to that role these all thing will get evaluated.

OIDC is the Identity federation.

This is best way for accessing the  like usernames/passwords , kubernetes secrets resource is actually not a secret it the encoded value not any encrypted
